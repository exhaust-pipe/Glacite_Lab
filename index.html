How the model works

The optimizer compares skipping, banking XP and targeting an item after observing each corpse. It maximizes long-run coins per mineshaft using a shared XP grid, weighted loot bundles and the full roll-count distribution.

Target weight increases with stored XP, up to 3×. The model resets XP after an early hit and spends the threshold on a guarantee that replaces one roll. It treats the dye separately. The estimate and bounds describe the discrete model.

Fairy shafts contain one Vanguard. Regular shafts use the selected spawn perks. Travel time, powder, order fills and price slippage are outside the coin objective. The Vanguard chestplate weight is unknown and starts at zero.
